How the stack operates

Structure: one primary carry / IGL / AWP (jsn789) + one secondary fragger (kpace) + three support players, with xvkt as a part-time AWP. jsn789 calls the strats — mostly standard rounds (spread across the map, playing off picks) — while also carrying entry, top fragging and the primary AWP load. The team wins on opening picks and utility, and struggles when firepower narrows to jsn789 and kpace alone. Priorities: grow xvkt into a reliable second AWP so jsn789 can focus on entry and calling, and build star rounds around the two main fraggers.
